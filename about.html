Our story

Our story

Where we come from.
What we are building.
Where we are going.

Rayudu Group began on 20 June 2023 with a clear and purposeful ambition — to build businesses that grow with time, create lasting value, and make a meaningful difference in people's lives.

What started as a single journey has steadily evolved into a diverse and connected business ecosystem, exploring opportunities across technology, trade, agriculture, finance, logistics, entertainment, and other areas that touch everyday life.

Today, Rayudu Group is entering its fourth year of operations, strengthening its foundations while continuing to explore new opportunities, build new businesses, and develop products that address evolving needs.

With a long-term vision to build 1,000+ products and applications, we continue to expand into new areas, embrace innovation, and build businesses designed to grow responsibly alongside the world.

This is more than our journey.
It is the future we are building.
